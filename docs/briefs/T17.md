## The trap

The CI container can quietly stay on the old version: a cached image tag, a second service block,
or an image line edited in the wrong job would leave CI green on 16, and production would then run
untested code paths on 18. That is why the version-check step must fail on anything other than
18, and why you report the line it printed from the CI log of your PR head.

Second trap: Docker's postgres:18 image moved its default data directory (`PGDATA`) compared with
16. A service container with no volume mount is unaffected. Do not add a volume or PGDATA
override; if the container fails to start, stop and report with the log rather than working around it.
